# Vistiq

## Register

product

## Users

Learners study English, look up knowledge, take exams and review their results. Admins and editors manage questions, exams, knowledge, reviews, users and system settings.

## Product Purpose

Provide connected learner and admin workflows in one Laravel application, with clear permissions and consistent interfaces.

## Brand Personality

Clear, compact and consistent, following the existing Vistiq interface and the rules in AGENTS.md.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing heroes, oversized headings, decorative statistics and promotional copy above admin working controls. Avoid feature-specific replacements for existing shared buttons, forms and notifications.

## Design Principles

- Put the current task and its controls first.
- Keep navigation, labels and component behavior consistent between modules.
- Preserve complete workflows on phones, tablets and desktops.
- Make loading, success, failure and empty states understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the shared UX rules in AGENTS.md: keyboard navigation, visible focus, labels and field errors, accessible control names, text status labels and reduced motion. Support touch input and narrow viewports.
